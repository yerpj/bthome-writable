Puck.js battery life against advertising interval, bthome-writable

Measured 2026-10-26 on a sealed Puck.js at 3.000 V with an Otii Arc Pro: floor 7.35 uA, one advertising event 14.19 uC, one command 1810 uC over 2.34 s, fast window 30 s at 100 ms. Life on 154 mAh, 70 % of a CR2032's 220 mAh.

The interval axis is the radio's own limit: the module refuses anything outside 20 ms to 10 s. Dotted lines are six months, one year and two years; the dots mark where each rate reaches one year. 726 days at one command a day and 10 s is the ceiling of everything the module can be set to do, so the two-year line is there to show that nothing reaches it. The 70 % is a flat allowance for what a coin cell does not give back (a 2.0 V rating, about 1 % a year of self-discharge, a rising internal resistance) and is not itself measured.
