The median is connection-bound. None of this makes a BLE connection faster; what it removes is the wait for a connectable advertising event, which was most of the 10.7 s.
The first command after a quiet period still pays the idle interval — 5–7 s at a 2 s advertising interval. That is the deliberate trade for battery, and where the remaining work is.
The advertising interval is not a latency dial. Measured at a deliberately slow 5 s: commands still land in 1.7 s. It sets the idle refresh rate and the cold start, nothing else.
